## Intent and boundary

Turn the 2026-06-11 deep audit into a remediation program: fix the real audio
path, demolish simulated and narration-heavy mass, consolidate hygiene, and
defer rebuilds to demand. Phase three added first-party stretch work after
the operator chose a Signal-owned time-stretch and pitch-shift engine over a
Rubber Band dependency. The July 2026 stretch audit findings (`g10.036`–
`g10.042`) were all closed. No batch was ready at close: every task ended
`complete`, `paused`, `deferred`, or `superseded`.

## Shipped outcomes

- Audio-path remediation: render-plane declick and playback correctness,
  output-stream hardening with real device enumeration, runtime rescope to an
  honest control plane, analysis pruning with measurement correctness
  (`g10.001`–`g10.006`).
- Domain pruning and consolidation: hosting-domain and plugin-domain
  demolition to real foundations, DSP corrections with polyphase resampling,
  workspace consolidation with truthful front doors (`g10.004`, `g10.007`–
  `g10.009`).
- Engine build-out on the surviving seed: graph-shaped plans, stable node
  identity, parameter fast path with automation, DSP kit, RT observability,
  WYSIWYG bounce, output-time honesty, recording capture, disk streaming,
  transport regions, runtime endgame (`g10.010`–`g10.020`).
- Stretch evidence and contracts: real-corpus benchmarks, first-party
  stretch evidence, offline artifact scale, product-workflow checkpoint
  (`g10.021`–`g10.025`, with `025` deferred — see below).
- RealtimePreview tier: callback-safe state and source-projected callback
  shipped (`g10.026`, `g10.027`); source-fill contract folded into `g10.040`
  (`g10.028`, superseded); stretch-correctness gate and consolidation
  ledgers closed (`g10.029` superseded historical ledger, `g10.030`
  complete).
- Creative stretch: exact fixed-ratio neutral `Dream` at `4x`/`8x`/`16x`
  made public, exact-ratio `Cyclic` at `2x`/`4x`/`8x` admitted, continuous
  Dream `4N..=16N` and continuous Cyclic `2N..=8N` published, Automatic
  same-character routing tried and closed with explicit characters retained
  (`g10.031`–`g10.035`; Contract `085`).
- Stretch audit recovery: Transparent correctness (overlap coverage, dynamic
  pitch, seam parity, output bound), cache identity `v3`, crate surface and
  evidence consolidation, resumable offline render admitted, RealtimePreview
  proven then deliberately left unadopted, `A18` transient-pop mechanism
  found and fixed with guard kept, pitch-aware resumable render with seam
  smoother removal (`g10.036`–`g10.042`; Contracts `084`, `046`).
