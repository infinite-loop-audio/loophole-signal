## Intent and boundary

Turn engine depth into a multi-consumer reusable product surface: explicit
public crate and schema boundaries, deeper multicore and anticipative
execution policy, Signal-owned orchestration for non-realtime work, broader
hardware and plugin portability without leaking host-local policy, and
release-ready conformance for Loophole, Finch, and later consumers. Stayed
inside Signal-owned boundaries: no product workflow, no Pulse/Aura-local
session semantics, no app export UX.

## Shipped outcomes

- Crate maturity, public contracts, and schema-freeze baseline (`g04.001`).
- Multicore graph scheduling and anticipative execution depth (`g04.002`).
- Runtime work orchestration and deferred-service policy (`g04.003`).
- Hardware backend portability and clock-domain boundary depth (`g04.004`).
- Plugin backend breadth and host-neutral delegation contracts (`g04.005`).
- Consumer conformance, export stability, and release packaging (`g04.006`).
