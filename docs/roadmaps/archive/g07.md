## Intent and boundary

Close the bounded feature-expansion queue around routing, Linux breadth,
control-surface substrate, and sample-domain transform services — all inside
Signal-owned runtime, adapter, hardware, and media substrate. Shaped in part
by repeated Chorus demand (engine spatial adapters, Linux device depth,
control-surface scripting). Stayed out of product-local controller UX.

## Shipped outcomes

- Routing and channel substrate: canonical multichannel layout and channel
  roles, sidechain and secondary-input execution, multi-bus graph execution,
  complex plugin I/O topology, spatial adapter execution baseline, surround
  bed/object/mix-policy expansion (`g07.001`–`g07.006`).
- Linux-native breadth: LV2 adapter baseline and Linux plugin lifecycle,
  cross-adapter parity and sandbox policy, ALSA/JACK/PipeWire backend
  portability, backend clocking/duplex/endpoint parity (`g07.007`–`g07.010`).
- Control substrate: external MIDI endpoints and device identity, MIDI 2.0
  and MPE expression, control-surface transport/mapping/feedback, advanced
  hardware extensibility and scripting-safe device policy (`g07.011`–
  `g07.014`).
- Time-stretch substrate: sample-domain stretch engine baseline, warp-marker
  and tempo-assist analysis, post-warp render cache and artifact depth,
  low-latency audition/scrub/preview services (`g07.015`–`g07.018`).
- Acceptance and closeout: multichannel/Linux/stretch/control-surface
  acceptance and the Loophole feature-readiness gate (`g07.019`–`g07.020`).

## Canonical destinations

Lasting routing, backend, and device behavior lives in the owning crates
under `docs/architecture/system-inventory.md`; lasting policy lives in

`g10` stretch program (Contracts `046`, `084`, `085`). Nothing in the removed
`docs/roadmaps/g07/` tree held the only statement of a live rule.
