## Intent and boundary

Turn the `g01` substrate into a deeper reusable analysis stack so Loophole,
Finch, and future consumers do not rebuild core algorithmic functionality in
app-local repos. Ordering: shared streaming spectral and resampling substrate
first, domain analyzers second, embedding/semantic inference after descriptor
surfaces stabilize, benchmarking and acceptance hardening last. Working rules
kept generic DSP/analysis in reusable crates, preferred shared offline and
streaming surfaces, kept realtime-safe pieces separate from offline-heavy
convenience layers, and validated against fixtures and corpus baselines.

## Shipped outcomes

- Streaming spectral, resampling, and analysis-rate substrate (`g02.001`).
- Rhythm structure, downbeat, and tempo-continuity depth (`g02.002`).
- Tonal analysis, tuning estimation, and harmonic tracking (`g02.003`).
- Loudness, true-peak, and multichannel dynamics depth (`g02.004`).
- Transient, timbral, and descriptor feature packs (`g02.005`).
- Embedding and semantic-analysis inference baseline (`g02.006`).
- Analysis corpus, benchmarking, and acceptance harnesses (`g02.007`).
